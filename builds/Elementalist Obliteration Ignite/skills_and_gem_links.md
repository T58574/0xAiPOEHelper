# Skills & Gem Links Guide (Archdemon Crown + Standard Penance Brand)

This document details your active setup using **Archdemon Crown**, standard **Penance Brand**, **Runebinder**, and **Herald of Ash + Herald of Purity**.

---

## 💥 Active Main Setup (Helmet: Archdemon Crown)

* **`Penance Brand`** (Lvl 20, Q22) — Base phys damage 4,400–6,600.
* **`Swiftbrand Support`** (Lvl 20/20) — **Crucial for activation speed!** Boosts activation frequency by ~50-59%.
* **`Cruelty Support`** (Lvl 20/20) — Up to 40% MORE Damage over Time.
* **`Burning Damage Support`** (Lvl 20/20) — 35% MORE Fire Damage over Time.

> [!NOTE]
> **Archdemon Crown Synergies**:
> - `+2 to Level of Socketed Skill Gems` raises Penance Brand to L22, boosting base physical hit damage significantly.
> - `Socketed Gems deal 30% more Elemental Damage` (Essence of Horror suffix) acts as an inherent 5th link!

---

## 🔮 Active Aura & Debuff Setups

### Auras & Heralds (Reserve Setup)
* **`Herald of Ash`** (Lvl 20) — Extra Fire Damage & burning overkill clear.
* **`Herald of Purity`** (Lvl 20) — Flat Physical Damage boost for Penance Brand's base hit.
* **`Flesh and Stone`** (Lvl 17) — Defensive aura.

### Boss Debuff Engine (Arcanist Brand / Self-Cast)
* **`Flammability`** — Reduces enemy Fire Resistance by -17% to -35%.
* **`Flame Surge`** — Creates Burning Ground under ignited bosses dealing 25% of your Ignite DPS as bonus damage.

---

## ⚡ Passive Tree Synergies

* **`Runebinder`** (Allocated):
  * Allows **2 Penance Brands to attach to 1 boss**.
  * Both brands build energy simultaneously, reaching the 20-energy max stack hit twice as fast!
