## ⚡ Why Archdemon Crown + Standard Penance Brand is Great

1. **Archdemon Crown 30% MORE Ele Damage Suffix**:
   - The Essence of Horror suffix (*Socketed Gems deal 30% More Elemental Damage*) combined with *+2 to Level of Socketed Skill Gems* makes your Archdemon Crown perform like a pseudo 6-link!
   - You do NOT need to spend currency on a 6-link body armour or replace this helmet.

2. **Standard Penance Brand Massive Base Hit**:
   - Standard Penance Brand grants **4,400 to 6,600 base physical damage** at Level 20.
   - Scaling standard Penance Brand to 20 energy stages yields a gargantuan physical/elemental hit, which `Shaper of Flames` converts into a massive single Ignite!
   - Saves 80 Chaos by keeping standard Penance Brand instead of buying `Dissipation`.

3. **`Runebinder` + `Swiftbrand` Energy Acceleration**:
   - With **`Runebinder`** allocated, 2 brands attach to a boss simultaneously.
   - 2 brands build energy 2x faster, reaching the maximum 20-energy stage explosion in half the time!
   - Adding **`Swiftbrand Support` (`Быстрое клеймо`)** further speeds up activation frequency by ~50%.

## 🛠️ Budget-Friendly Upgrade Priorities (Under 20 Chaos)

1. **`Swiftbrand Support` (2–3 Chaos)**:
   - Socket into Archdemon Crown to boost brand activation frequency.
2. **Quality on Support Gems (1–2 Chaos each)**:
   - Upgrade `Cruelty`, `Burning Damage`, and `Combustion` to 20% Quality.
3. **`Flammability` Curse (1 Chaos)**:
   - Self-cast or linked with `Arcanist Brand` on bosses to shred enemy Fire Resistance.
4. **Bench-Craft Gloves (4 Alchemy)**:
   - Craft `+% Fire Damage over Time Multiplier` on open prefix.
